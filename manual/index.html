Manual

One phrase, four playheads, for the M-VAVE FM-1. One of six sequencer firmwares built from a survey of sequencer archetypes (see the companion survey). Built on fm1-base; makes its own sound and sends MIDI.

What it is for

Archetype: Multi-playhead / Cartesian: Fugue Machine, PolyPhase, Make Noise Rene.

CANON-4 turns one short phrase into counterpoint. Sixteen cells (a 4 x 4 field of scale degrees and rests, on the white keys) are read by four playheads at once, each at its own rate and direction, along its own path through the field (rows, columns, a snake, diagonals, a spiral, at random), from its own offset and length, transposed by its own degrees and played by its own sound. The same tune a third above at half speed, backwards an octave up, a slow bass on the columns: one edit and every voice hears it. The heads drift apart and meet again every few bars, so the variation always resolves.

What it does

  • 16 cells: degree, note or rest, velocity, chance, length.
  • 4 heads: rate 1/32..1/2, direction, path (6), offset, length 1-16, transpose in degrees, octave, sound, mute.
  • Heads meet again every N bars (or run free); FX or black key 11 re-syncs now.
  • Tap a cell key: note or rest; hold it: the knobs are the cell.
  • REC: the keys write the cells one after another; dice with undo; key and scale with SEL.
  • The screen: the field with each head's position as a coloured corner, and the four heads' settings.

Install

Use the installer page in Chrome or Edge with the FM-1 connected by USB. Keep M-VAVE's stock firmware and M-UPGRADE at hand: the installer can put it back. To build it yourself, see the source.

Status

0.1, untested on hardware. It builds for the FM-1 and passes its host-simulator tests; nothing has been run on a real FM-1 yet.
